Off-site catering

Old Europe, at your address

Add a little traditional German flavour to your next event. We cater off site for parties from 25 guests to well over 100, with food prepared fresh that day and, if you like, grilled on site.

Whatever your budget: linen and porcelain, or something easy with disposable plates.

We have catered for: Embassy of Austria · EU Open House at the German Embassy · Walter Reed · Neighbourhood festivals in Glover Park. References gladly provided.

Call and ask for Alex catering@old-europe.com

  • Deutsche Wurst

    Bratwurst, Bauernwurst, Weisswurst, Frankfurter, Wiener, Debreziner, Nürnberger, Landjäger, Knackwurst.

  • Roasts and classics

    Sauerbraten, Schweinenackenbraten, Kassler, Rindsroulade, Tafelspitz, Königsberger Klopse, three kinds of goulash.

  • Schnitzel and chicken

    Wiener Art, Jägerschnitzel, Black Forest chicken, Backhähnchen, Geschnetzeltes in Riesling cream.

  • Sides and salads

    German potato salad, apple red cabbage, sauerkraut, spätzle, Kartoffelpuffer, cucumber dill, Wurstsalat.

  • Finger food and platters

    Canapés, mini quiche, Frikadellen, asparagus ham rolls, cheese with Obazda, cold cuts, house-cured salmon.

  • Desserts

    Apfelstrudel with vanilla sauce, Black Forest cake, Sacher Torte, Bavarian cream, Rote Grütze.

Seasonal items are subject to availability, and all menu items are subject to change.
