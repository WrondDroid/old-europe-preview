Zum Mitnehmen

Takeout and delivery

Most of the menu travels well. Call during opening hours to order for pickup or delivery; ordering earlier in the day lets us reserve your delivery time.

Call to order · (202) 333-7600 See the menu

Wednesday to Sunday · Closed Monday and Tuesday.

Delivery

  • Free delivery within a 5-mile radius.
  • Beyond 5 miles we kindly ask for a minimum of two entrées.
  • A 20% gratuity is added to all delivery orders.
  • Delivery depends on staffing on the day, so please call first.

Beer, wine and spirits to go

  • German beer, wine and spirits to take home (21 and over).
  • German draft beer to go: one litre $15, two litres $25, while supplies last.

A “mystery” beer on us

  • Takeout and delivery orders over $75 before tax: one free beer.
  • Orders over $150 before tax: two free beers.
  • While supplies last.
