German food and culture, twelve months a year

The year at Old Europe

Every season and every major holiday gets its own menu. Dates change from year to year, so please call for this season’s details.

Winter

  • Gulasch Woche · Goulash Week

    A week of goulash specialties to take the edge off the cold.

  • Wild · Wild game

    Venison, wild boar and Hasenpfeffer with savoury cabbage, house-made soups and hot mulled Glühwein.

  • Valentine’s Day

    A special menu for two.

Spring

  • St. Patrick’s Day

    A holiday menu for our neighbours.

  • Ostern · Easter

    A festive Easter menu.

  • Spargelzeit · Asparagus season

    From March to June, spring means asparagus and May wine.

  • Tag des Deutschen Bieres · German Beer Day

    A toast to the Reinheitsgebot.

  • Muttertag · Mother’s Day

    Bring the whole family.

Summer

  • Vatertag · Father’s Day

    A menu for Dad.

  • Summer Grillfest

    Grilled meats and seafood, delicate wheat beers and refreshing chilled soups.

Autumn

  • Oktoberfest

    A rollicking Bavarian atmosphere, Bock beer and hearty fare when the leaves start to fall.

  • Weihnachten · Christmas

    A traditional Christmas menu.

  • Silvester · New Year’s Eve

    See the old year out at the table.

Events are subject to change. Follow @oldeuroperestaurant_dc on Instagram for updates and promotions.
